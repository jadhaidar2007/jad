# Canada Pet Store — True Dropshipping

## Model
True dropshipping (no inventory held), targeting Canada, selling via Shopify
with an existing payment gateway already in place.

## Why Canada (not Lebanon COD, the original plan)
Jad has a payment gateway ready and can get a site live immediately in
Canada — removes the fulfillment/local-presence bottleneck that was the main
blocker for the Lebanon COD plan (Jad has no local presence there, studies in
Bremen). Trade-off knowingly accepted: higher CPMs/CPCs and more competition
than Lebanon, but faster to launch and lower operational risk (no local 3PL/
courier relationship needed, no COD non-collection risk).

## Niche
Pet products — decided over home/kitchen gadgets because of stronger
repeat-purchase potential (consumables), higher emotional/impulse buying
behavior, and lower comparison-shopping risk on niche pet items vs. generic
kitchen gadgets.

## Funnel structure
Hero product + aggressive upsell, not a full multi-SKU catalog store. One
strong hero for the ad/landing page, complementary items offered as
cart/post-purchase upsells (bundle framing: "complete the set," not a hard
sell).

### Hero candidates
Lick mat and/or snuffle mat — both cheap ($2-5 landed), high markup (60%+
margins typical), proven demand across multiple independent sources
(TikTok/Instagram traction, repeat "best seller" mentions), strong visual
demo potential.

### Planned upsell set (complementary, same use-case)
- Slow-feeder bowl
- Second lick mat (different design/flavor)
- Grooming glove/deshedding brush
- Paw cleaner

Final selection pending supplier sourcing.

## Sourcing requirement
Must use a supplier with confirmed North America (US/Canada) warehousing —
not standard China-direct AliExpress shipping. Reason: true dropshipping +
card payment (not COD) means slow shipping (10-20+ days from China) creates
real chargeback/dispute risk. Target: 3-7 day delivery via CJ Dropshipping,
Zendrop, or similar NA-warehoused supplier.

## Budget
~$300 test capital. Given Canadian ad costs, this buys a short, thin test
window — expectations calibrated accordingly (a few days of real spend, not
weeks).

## Timeline commitment
Jad has committed to a focused one-month test of this specific plan before
reassessing. Store + gateway can be live within days since infrastructure
already exists.

## Messaging caution
Carried over from earlier research: avoid leaning on "anxiety/calming/
therapeutic" claims (common in lick mat/pet-calming product marketing) —
same ad-policy risk flagged earlier for a human sensory product; keep
framing practical/fun, not medical/emotional-support adjacent.

## Standing evaluation criteria
Jad's own rules, apply to all future product checks:
1. Can it sell generically/without private branding (no brand-trust
   dependency)?
2. Can complementary/upsell products be identified alongside it?

## Immediate next steps (in order)
1. Confirm Shopify store + payment gateway is fully live.
2. Choose lick mat vs. snuffle mat as hero (or test both) — check Meta Ad
   Library filtered to Canada for ad longevity (3+ weeks running = stronger

   Dropshipping/Zendrop) — confirm actual shipping times before committing.
4. Order 1 sample of the hero for quality check + real demo footage (not

5. Set fixed daily ad spend cap in writing before first ad goes live.
6. Launch ads within days of store being ready — avoid further research

7. Track CPA/CTR/conversion daily; decide kill-or-scale based on real data
   at the end of the test window, not sunk cost.
