# Clipping Outreach Kit

Templates for landing paid clipping deals — the fastest path to actual money,
since it pays regardless of your own follower count (see README for why).
Copy, tweak the bracketed parts, send.

---

## 1. Who to target

Best first targets (easiest yes, fastest reply):
- Podcasters/streamers with **10k–200k** audience — big enough to have clip-worthy
  content, small enough that they don't already have a clipping team and will
  actually read a cold DM.
- Twitch streamers who go live 3+ hours regularly — huge amount of raw footage,
  chronically short-form-content-starved.
- YouTubers doing long-form interviews/podcasts who post to YouTube only and
  have zero TikTok/IG/Shorts presence — you're pointing at pure upside they're
  leaving on the table.

Where to find them: YouTube search + sort by upload frequency, Twitch directory,
podcast guest-booking sites, or just scroll niches you already know.

---

## 2. Cold DM (Twitter/X, Instagram, Discord)

Short version — DMs get read in 3 seconds, don't write a pitch deck:

> Hey [name] — big fan of [specific episode/moment, be real]. I run an AI
> clipping pipeline that turns your long-form into 10 short-form clips
> (TikTok/Shorts/Reels, captioned, ready to post) same day. Free trial clip
> from your last upload so you can see the quality — want me to send it?

Why this works: leads with a *free sample*, zero ask, low friction. Most
replies come from actually sending the clip, not the pitch.

## 3. Cold email (longer-form, for podcast/business inquiries)

**Subject:** Turning [Show Name] into daily short-form — free sample clip

> Hi [Name],
>
> I've been listening to [Show Name] and think a few recent moments — [name
> one specific clip-worthy moment] — would do really well as short-form.
>
> I run a clipping pipeline (AI-assisted, fully edited: captions, reframed to
> vertical, hook-first) that can turn one long-form episode into ~10
> platform-ready clips posted across TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram
> Reels, typically same day.
>
> Rather than pitch you on a retainer up front, I'd like to send you a free
> sample clip from your latest episode so you can judge the quality yourself.
> If it's good, we can talk about an ongoing arrangement — if not, no hard
> feelings and you're out nothing.
>
> Worth a shot?
>
> [Your name]

## 4. The free sample — non-negotiable

Always lead with a free, unsolicited clip before asking for money. It's the
single biggest lever for conversion in this business: it proves quality
instead of claiming it, and it's now nearly free for you to produce since the
engine does it in minutes. Pick their best/most recent upload, run it through
the pipeline, send the top 1-2 clips with no ask attached.

---

## 5. Pricing / deal structures (pick based on their size)

| Structure | When to use | Typical rate |
|---|---|---|
| **Per-clip flat fee** | New/small creator, one-off | $5–50/clip depending on their audience size |
| **Per-view (pay-per-performance)** | Larger creator, they want no upfront risk | $0.02–0.10 per 1,000 views generated (test a batch first) |
| **Monthly retainer** | Established podcast/streamer, recurring content | $500–5,000/mo for a set number of clips/week across all 3 platforms |
| **Rev-share on a joint channel** | You want upside instead of a flat fee | You run a co-branded clips account, split ad/creator-fund revenue 50/50 or similar |

Start every new relationship on **per-clip or per-view** — it's the lowest-risk
ask for them to say yes to. Move to a retainer once they've seen a few weeks
of consistent output.

---

## 6. Follow-up cadence

- No reply after the free clip → one follow-up after 4–5 days: "Did the clip
  land ok? Happy to do another from a different episode if that one wasn't
  the right fit."
- After that, drop it. Don't chase past 2 touches — move to the next target.
- Track everything in a simple sheet: creator, channel size, date contacted,
  sample sent?, reply, deal status. (Ask me to build this as a spreadsheet if
  you want it tracked properly.)

---

## 7. What to say once they say yes

Get these three things locked before you start producing at volume:
1. **Rights confirmation** — explicit written OK (even a DM saying "yes go
   ahead") that you can clip and repost their content. This is what makes the
   whole operation legal and eligible for platform payouts.
2. **Branding preferences** — do they want their handle/logo watermarked on
   clips, specific hashtags, tone/style notes.
3. **Posting cadence + platforms** — how many clips/week, which of
   TikTok/Shorts/Reels, whose account posts them (yours tagging them, or
   directly to their own accounts if you have their login/API access).

Feed those into `ENABLED_PLATFORMS`/captions in the engine config per client
once you're running more than one creator through it.
