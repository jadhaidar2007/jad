# Whop Partner Program — Start-to-$50/day Plan

## Two different Whop programs (don't mix these up)
- **Whop Affiliates** — you refer *buyers* to someone else's existing whop (community/product), earn a cut of that sale. One-off, not what this plan is about.
- **Whop Partners** — you refer *businesses* onto the Whop platform. You earn up to **30% of Whop's own processing fee** (~2.9% of everything that business processes) for 6 months minimum, extending as their volume grows. **This is the program in the article, and the one this plan builds.**

Apply / read the real terms here: https://docs.whop.com/manage-your-business/growth-marketing/affiliate-program (Whop's docs) — confirm current partner terms and get your referral link before outreach, since payout %, cap, and duration can change.

## The math (use this to qualify leads, don't skip it)
monthly processing × 2.9% = Whop's fee → × 30% = your monthly commission

| Their monthly processing | Your monthly commission |
|---|---|
| $25k | ~$218 |
| $50k | ~$435 |
| $100k | ~$870 |
| $250k | ~$2,175 |

**Floor: don't chase anything under ~$50k/mo processing.** Same effort to pitch a $25k business as a $250k one.

**Your $50/day target** ≈ $1,500/mo ≈ **3-4 closed businesses averaging $50-80k/mo processing**, OR fewer bigger ones. Realistic timeline: first commission lands 4-8 weeks after your first solid conversation (their migration takes weeks, then a payout cycle). So the $50/day plan is really "10 conversations a day starting tomorrow, so money starts landing in 4-8 weeks and compounds after."

## Tomorrow morning, in order
1. **Get your Whop Partner link/dashboard set up first** — nothing else matters until you can hand someone a real referral link. Sign up as a Partner in the Whop dashboard, confirm current commission %, and get the exact link format.
2. **Pick ONE market to start** (see LEADS_SOURCING.md) — don't spread across all 4 on day one. Recommendation: **local service businesses** (least competition, see article) or **LatAm Hotmart sellers** (biggest fee-gap pitch) — pick whichever matches a language/niche you already know.
3. **Build a list of 20 prospects** today using LEADS_SOURCING.md, sized by your $50k/mo floor.
4. **Send 20 outreach messages** using OUTREACH_TEMPLATES.md (matched to the right "door" per prospect).
5. **Log every one in tracker.csv** — name, contact, channel size/est. processing, door used, date, reply status.
6. Repeat steps 3-5 daily. Track reply rate; after ~3 days, drop whichever channel/door is under-performing and double down on what's replying.

## The 3 "doors" — pick per prospect before writing anything
- **Door 1 (fee gap):** they're on Hotmart (~10%), Substack (~9%), or a legacy card terminal (2.6-3.5% + hardware rental). Pitch: concrete dollar savings per month.
- **Door 2 (tool sprawl):** they're stitching together separate checkout + subscription tool + Discord bot + manual payouts. Pitch: one account, hours back.
- **Door 3 (more customers):** their only acquisition channel is their own audience. Pitch: Whop's built-in discovery/affiliate tooling.

## Markets to work (in priority order for a fast start)
1. Local service businesses (detailers, barbers, mobile mechanics, trainers) — least crowded, tap-to-pay pitch is simple and visual.
2. LatAm info-product sellers on Hotmart — biggest, most quantifiable fee-gap pitch (~$7k/mo savings at $100k/mo processing).
3. Paid online communities (trading/education Discords) — public member counts + price = instant volume estimate.
4. Ecommerce brands with real ad spend/review velocity.

Skip restaurants (POS lock-in, thin margins, slow).

## Daily operating rhythm once running
- Morning: pull 15-20 new prospects, qualify against the $50k floor.
- Midday: send outreach batch, respond to any replies same-day (speed matters more than polish).
- Afternoon: any prospect who's interested → build them a quick demo store page, screen-record a 2-3 min walkthrough, send it.
- Evening: update tracker.csv, note who to follow up with in 4-5 days.

## Reality check on timing
- First commission: expect 4-8 weeks, not days — it needs a real conversation → them migrating → a payout cycle.
- This is a volume + patience game: stacking businesses is what gets you to $50/day, not any single deal.
- Concentration risk: you depend on Whop's terms. Confirm current rates before quoting numbers to prospects.
