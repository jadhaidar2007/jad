# Where to actually find prospects (free, no scraping tools needed)

Use these manually tomorrow to build your first list of 20. Estimate monthly
processing from public signals (member count × price, review velocity, ad
presence) before reaching out — this is your $50k/mo floor filter.

## 1. Local service businesses (recommended starting market)
- **Google Maps**: search "[service] near [city]" (e.g. "mobile detailing Austin",
  "personal trainer Miami"). Filter for businesses with 50+ reviews and a
  website/Instagram — signals real, steady volume.
- **Instagram/TikTok location + hashtag search**: `#mobiledetailing`,
  `#personaltrainer`, `#mobilemechanic` — look for ones already taking
  bookings/payments online (Calendly + card link = existing friction to fix).
- Estimate volume: ask yourself "how many clients/week at what price" — a
  detailer doing 15 jobs/week at $150 is ~$9k/mo (too small alone; bundle a
  few, or look for multi-van/multi-location operators to hit $50k+).

## 2. LatAm info-product sellers on Hotmart (biggest fee-gap pitch)
- **Hotmart Market** (hotmart.com/market) — Hotmart's own public marketplace
  lists top products with sales/ranking signals by category (courses,
  education, coaching).
- X/Twitter search operators: `"link en bio" curso` or `hotmart lang:es` to
  find sellers actively promoting.
- Instagram: search Spanish/Portuguese hashtags like `#cursoonline`,
  `#infoproductos`, `#hotmart` — bios often link straight to their checkout,
  which tells you their current price point and (from launch posts) rough
  volume.

## 3. Paid online communities (Discord/trading/education)
- **DISBOARD** (disboard.org/servers/tag/trading, /tag/forex, /tag/investing,
  /tag/day-trading) — public Discord directory, filter by tag, member counts
  are visible on each server's page.
- **Disbrowse** (disbrowse.com) — similar directory, different index/coverage.
  Cross-check both since neither is complete.
- Estimate volume: member count × visible subscription price (many list price
  in server description or a linked Whop/Stripe checkout).

## 4. Ecommerce brands
- Instagram/TikTok shop tabs in your niche — check review velocity, comment
  volume, and whether they're running ads (visible via the "Sponsored" tag or
  Meta Ad Library: facebook.com/ads/library — search their brand name).
- Consistent daily ad spend + steady reviews = real recurring processing
  volume worth qualifying further.

## Qualifying fast (don't overthink it)
For each candidate, get to a rough monthly-processing number in under 2
minutes using visible signals (price × rough volume/reviews/members). If you
can't get within a reasonable range of $50k/mo from public info, either look
for a bigger account in the same niche or move on — don't guess-invest time
in a maybe.

## What NOT to do
- Don't scrape or mass-DM in ways that get you banned from a platform — every
  channel above supports manual, personalized outreach at the volume this
  plan needs (10-20/day).
- Don't quote exact commission numbers to a prospect until you've confirmed
  current Whop Partner terms in your own dashboard — rates can change.
