# Outreach templates — match the door + market, then send

Fill in `[brackets]`. Keep it short — these read as a real message, not a
pitch deck. Always end with one clear, low-friction question.

## Local service businesses (Door: tap-to-pay / fee + hardware)

**Instagram/X DM:**
> Hey [name] — saw [business] does a ton of bookings, love the [specific
> detail from their page]. Quick one: are you still renting a card terminal
> for payments? I help shops like yours move to tap-to-pay on your phone,
> no hardware rental, lower fee than most terminals. Worth a 2-min look?

## LatAm Hotmart sellers (Door: fee gap — biggest number to quote)

**X/Instagram DM (Spanish):**
> Hola [nombre] — vi tu curso en Hotmart, se ve muy bien. Una pregunta
> rápida: ¿sabías que Hotmart cobra ~10% y hay plataformas que cobran ~2.9%?
> En un curso que factura $100k/mes eso es como $7,000 de diferencia al mes.
> Te mando un cálculo rápido con tus números?

**English version (if they reply in English):**
> Hey [name] — saw your course on Hotmart, looks great. Quick question: did
> you know Hotmart charges ~10% while some platforms run closer to ~2.9%?
> On a $100k/month course that's roughly $7,000/month difference. Want me to
> run the numbers on your actual volume?

## Universal follow-up (any market, day 4-5, no reply)
> Hey [name] — following up in case this got buried. No pressure either
> way, just let me know if it's worth a quick look or not the right time.

**After 2 touches with no reply: stop, move to the next prospect.**

---

## Once they say yes — lock these 3 things before building anything
1. Rough current processing volume (confirms they clear the $50k/mo floor
   and lets you quote real commission math to yourself — never to them
   beyond "this is cheaper" framing).
2. What they're currently using (Hotmart / Stripe+separate tools / terminal)
   — tells you which door actually landed and how to frame the demo.
3. A time for a screen-share/video walkthrough of a demo store page you
   build for them.
